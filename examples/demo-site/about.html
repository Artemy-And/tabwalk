About us

We started in a small workshop in 2012. Read our story or watch the video.

Every kettle and pan is pressed, polished and tested by hand. We make a few hundred a month and repair anything we have ever sold, for as long as you own it. Spare lids, handles and seals are kept in stock for every model since the first one.

Our aprons are cut from linen woven two streets away. Orders placed before noon leave the same day, wrapped in paper and string rather than plastic, and every parcel carries a card signed by the person who packed it.
